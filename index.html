RideTrack Support

Help with RideTrack Moto for iPhone and Apple Watch

Questions, bugs or feature ideas: email sammymittman@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing, and a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Rides stop recording when the screen is off. Open Settings › RideTrack › Location and choose Always.

My bike battery won't connect. RideTrack reads smart BMS packs over Bluetooth (Talaria, eWatt and packs with ANT, JBD/Xiaoxiang, JK, Daly or Greenway boards). Stock Sur-Ron, E Ride Pro, Stark and Segway batteries have no Bluetooth. Make sure the bike is on and no other app is connected to the pack.

Heart rate doesn't show. Install RideTrack on your Apple Watch, turn on Heart Rate & Wrist Sensors in the You tab, and allow Health access on the watch.

Group rides, friends or leaderboards say iCloud is unavailable. Sign in to iCloud in the Settings app and turn on iCloud Drive.

Restore my Pro purchase. Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Get Pro free. Share your invite code from the Pro screen. Each friend counts once they download RideTrack, enter your code and finish their first ride. Five friends unlocks Pro.
