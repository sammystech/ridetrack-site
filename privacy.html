RideTrack Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

RideTrack records your rides on electric dirt bikes, gas dirt bikes and mountain bikes. It is built to keep your data on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Location. GPS tracks your speed, distance and route during a ride, including with the screen off if you allow background location. Routes are saved only on your device. Your live location is shared only during a group ride you join (see below).
  • Motion. The accelerometer, gyroscope and barometer detect jumps, launches, climbing and when you've stopped. Motion data is processed on the device and is never uploaded.
  • Bike battery (Bluetooth). If you connect your bike's smart battery, RideTrack reads its charge, voltage, current and temperature to show range. It only reads. It never changes your battery's settings, and the readings stay on your device.
  • Rides, trails, launch times and pins are stored in the app on your iPhone. If you have iCloud Backup turned on, Apple includes them in your device backup.
  • Photos. RideTrack only adds the share cards you choose to save to your photo library. It doesn't read your existing photos.
  • Apple Watch. The Watch app shows your live ride and sends Start, Pause and Finish to your iPhone. Nothing goes anywhere else.

Heart rate and Apple Health

If you wear an Apple Watch and allow Health access, RideTrack runs a cycling workout on the watch during each ride. The watch reads your heart rate, active calories and wrist motion (to help catch jumps) and sends them only to RideTrack on your iPhone, where the average and max heart rate and calories are saved with the ride. The workout is saved to Apple Health, so it shows in the Fitness app.

Health data never leaves your devices through RideTrack. It isn't uploaded, shared, posted to leaderboards, or used for advertising or marketing. You can turn this off under You › Accessories › Heart Rate & Wrist Sensors, and change Health access any time in the Health app or iPhone Settings › Health.

What you choose to share

Two optional community features use Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Nothing is shared unless you turn it on or post it yourself.

  • Leaderboards are off by default. If you turn them on, each saved ride posts a score: the rider name you pick, your bike type, distance, top speed, ride time, jump count, biggest air and the date. Your route and location are never included.
  • Group rides are something you start or join with a code. While you are recording a ride in a group, your current location, speed, distance, top speed, jump count, biggest air and rider name are shared every few seconds with the riders in that group through iCloud. Nothing is shared when you aren't recording, and leaving the group removes your entry. Groups end on their own after 12 hours.
  • Friends are added with a link or QR code you share. A friend can see the leaderboard scores you post, never your routes.
  • Invite codes. If you enter a friend's invite code, then after your first ride a record is saved with that code, an anonymous ID for your iCloud account and the ride's date, so your friend can be credited. It holds no route, location or name.
  • Spots are riding spots you choose to post: a name, notes, type, difficulty and the map location you pick. Other riders can see them.
  • Reports. If you report a spot or score, the report and your reason are sent so it can be reviewed.

Shared items carry the rider name you choose (use a nickname) and an anonymous iCloud ID that Apple creates just for RideTrack. We never see your real name, email or Apple ID. Turning leaderboard posting off takes your rides off the leaderboards, and Remove Everything Online in Settings deletes everything you've shared. Riders can also share a spot privately with friends through an iCloud link.

Purchases

RideTrack Pro and its free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked.

Crash and performance reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. They contain no ride, location or personal data.

Children

RideTrack is not directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Change or revoke Location, Motion, Bluetooth, Health and Photos access any time in iPhone Settings › RideTrack.
  • Delete rides in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone.
  • Delete your shared spots and scores any time with Remove Everything Online in the app's Settings.
