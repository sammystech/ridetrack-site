RideTrack Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

RideTrack records your rides on electric dirt bikes, gas dirt bikes and mountain bikes. It is built to keep your data on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone, except what you choose to share with other riders as described below.

RideTrack is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"), who is responsible for the information described here. You can reach us at the address at the end of this policy.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Location. GPS tracks your speed, distance and route during a ride, including with the screen off if you allow background location. Routes are saved only on your device. Your live location is shared only during a group ride you join (see below).
  • Motion. The accelerometer, gyroscope and barometer detect jumps, launches, climbing and when you've stopped. Motion data is processed on the device and is never uploaded.
  • Bike battery (Bluetooth). If you connect your bike's smart battery, RideTrack reads its charge, voltage, current and temperature to show range. It only reads. It never changes your battery's settings, and the readings stay on your device.
  • Rides, trails, launch times and pins are stored in the app on your iPhone. If you have iCloud Backup turned on, Apple includes them in your device backup.
  • Photos. RideTrack only adds the share cards you choose to save to your photo library. It doesn't read your existing photos.
  • Apple Watch. The Watch app shows your live ride and sends Start, Pause and Finish to your iPhone. Nothing goes anywhere else.

Heart rate and Apple Health

If you wear an Apple Watch and allow Health access, RideTrack runs a cycling workout on the watch during each ride. The watch reads your heart rate, active calories and wrist motion (to help catch jumps) and sends them only to RideTrack on your iPhone, where the average and max heart rate and calories are saved with the ride. The workout is saved to Apple Health, so it shows in the Fitness app.

Health data never leaves your devices through RideTrack. It isn't uploaded, shared, posted to leaderboards, or used for advertising or marketing. You can turn this off under You › Accessories › Heart Rate & Wrist Sensors, and change Health access any time in the Health app or iPhone Settings › Health.

What you choose to share

The optional community features use Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). Nothing is shared unless you turn it on or post it yourself.

  • Leaderboards are off by default. If you turn them on, each saved ride posts a score: the rider name you pick, your bike type, distance, top speed, ride time, jump count, biggest air and the date. Your route and location are never included. If you're signed in to Game Center, your bests are also sent to RideTrack's Game Center leaderboards, where Apple shows them under your Game Center name.
  • Group rides are something you start or join with a code. While you are recording a ride in a group, your current location, speed, distance, top speed, jump count, biggest air and rider name are shared every few seconds with the riders in that group through iCloud. Nothing is shared when you aren't recording, and leaving the group removes your entry. Groups end on their own after 12 hours.
  • Public trails. If you make one of your trails public, its name, its line on the map (start to finish), its length, the date and your rider name are posted so other riders can ride it. Riders on the leaderboards who ride a public trail post their time on it: rider name, the time, the ride's date and an anonymous ride ID, never the rest of the route. Making a trail private again removes it and every time on it. A public trail shows exactly where it starts and ends, so never make public a trail that starts or ends at your home.
  • Game Center (optional). If you sign in with Game Center from the leaderboards, your Game Center nickname becomes your rider name and your Game Center player ID for RideTrack is saved with what you post, so other riders see your name is verified. Your Game Center photo is only shown on your phone. RideTrack does not read your Game Center friends. You can go back to a typed name any time in Settings.
  • Friends are added with a link or QR code you share. A friend can see the leaderboard scores you post, never your routes.
  • Invite codes. If you enter a friend's invite code, then after your first ride a record is saved with that code, an anonymous ID for your iCloud account and the ride's date, so your friend can be credited. It holds no route, location or name.
  • Spots are riding spots you choose to post: a name, notes, type, difficulty, the map location you pick and any photos you add. Other riders can see them. Don't add photos that show people's faces or license plates without their permission.
  • Reports. If you report a spot or score, the report and your reason are sent so it can be reviewed.

Shared items carry the rider name you choose (use a nickname, or your Game Center nickname if you verify with Game Center) and an anonymous iCloud ID that Apple creates just for RideTrack. We never see your real name, email or Apple ID. Turning leaderboard posting off takes your rides off the leaderboards, and Remove Everything Online in Settings deletes everything you've shared. Riders can also share a spot privately with friends through an iCloud link.

Where shared data is stored, and for how long

What you share is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) for RideTrack, on Apple's servers, which may be outside your country. Apple protects it under its own security and privacy terms. Shared items stay until you remove them, turn the feature off or use Remove Everything Online; group ride locations are deleted when the group ends (12 hours at most). Reports are kept as long as needed to review them. We never copy your data to our own servers.

No system is perfectly secure. Only share what you're comfortable with other riders seeing.

Purchases

RideTrack Pro and its free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked.

Weather for your rides

With RideTrack Pro, after you save a ride RideTrack asks Apple's WeatherKit service for the weather during it. It sends only the ride's approximate starting location (rounded to about 1 km) and its start and end times. No name, account or other identifier is sent, and the weather is stored with the ride on your iPhone. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

Crash and performance reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports. They contain no ride, location or personal data.

Children

RideTrack is not directed to children under 13. Riders under 13 shouldn't use friends, group rides, leaderboards, public trails or spots, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you're a parent and think your child under 13 has shared something, use Remove Everything Online on their iPhone or email us and we'll delete it. Riders under 18 should use RideTrack with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Your choices

  • Change or revoke Location, Motion, Bluetooth, Health and Photos access any time in iPhone Settings › RideTrack.
  • Delete rides in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone.
  • Delete your shared spots, trails and scores any time with Remove Everything Online in the app's Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, or object to how it's used. Almost everything is on your iPhone, under your control; for anything shared, use the controls above or email us and we'll help. We process shared data to provide the features you turn on (your request), and you can withdraw by turning them off. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
